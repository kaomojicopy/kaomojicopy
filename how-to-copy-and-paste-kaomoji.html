How to copy and paste kaomoji

Copying a kaomoji takes one tap, and pasting works the same way as any other text. Here are the steps for each device, and what to do when a face looks wrong.

Kaomoji copy paste: the quick way on this site

Find a face, then tap it. It is copied to your clipboard right away, and a small message confirms it. Then paste it where you want it. You can also tap the heart to save the face to your favorites.

iPhone and iPad

After copying, tap and hold in a text box and choose Paste. To use a favorite face often, you can add it as a text replacement in your keyboard settings, so a short word turns into the face. Settings names can change between iOS versions.

Android

After copying, long-press in a text field and tap Paste. Many Android keyboards also keep a clipboard of recent items, and some let you save a shortcut for a face in the personal dictionary. Exact menus depend on your keyboard app.

Windows and Mac

On a computer, paste with Ctrl+V on Windows or Cmd+V on a Mac. Recent versions of Windows also include a kaomoji tab in the emoji panel, which opens with the Windows key and the period key. On a Mac, you can save a face as a text replacement in keyboard settings.

Why a kaomoji shows boxes or looks broken

Boxes usually mean your device lacks a font for one of the symbols. Try another face, update your device, or switch to a different keyboard. Some apps also change the look of special marks, so a face may look slightly different from one app to another.

Why the shrug loses its arm

In apps such as Discord and Slack, a backslash before an underscore can be read as formatting. The shrug then loses its arm. Type a second backslash, or paste the face inside a code block, to fix it. You can find the shrug and other classics on the shrug emoticons page.

Using kaomoji with screen readers

Screen readers may read each symbol in a kaomoji out loud, which can sound long. Keep faces short, and put the important meaning in words. This keeps your message clear for everyone.
