What we collect

The site has no accounts, forms, or comments, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

What is stored on your device

The site saves your favorite faces and your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when you load the pages; see GitHub's own privacy statement for details. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google's servers.

Analytics and advertising

At the time of the last update, the site does not use analytics or show advertisements. If we add analytics or advertising services in the future, such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense, we will update this page to describe them, including any cookies they use and how you can opt out.

Children

The site is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
