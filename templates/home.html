What is kaomoji?

Kaomoji (顔文字) are Japanese-style faces made from text characters, like (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶) or (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧. The word combines the Japanese words for "face" and "character". Unlike emoji, which are tiny pictures, kaomoji are built from letters, punctuation, and symbols, so you can paste them anywhere you can type text.

How to copy and paste kaomoji

Find a face by mood or search, then tap it. It is copied to your clipboard right away. Paste it with Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on a Mac), or long-press in a text box on your phone and choose Paste. Tap the heart on a face to keep it in your favorites.

Where can you use kaomoji?

In chats and direct messages, social media comments and bios, forum posts, emails, notes, and documents. Most apps that accept plain text will accept kaomoji too, though a few faces may look slightly different depending on the fonts on your device.
