About Kaomoji Copy

Kaomoji Copy is a small, free site for finding and copying kaomoji, the Japanese-style text faces like (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶). Pick a mood, tap a face, and paste it anywhere you type.

What you will find here

The library has 331 faces sorted into 19 collections, such as happy, love, cute, sad, sleepy, sassy, angry, shy, cats, bears, bunnies, shrug, lenny face, table flip, meme faces, and aesthetic sparkles. Each face has a short label, so you can also search by feeling, like "blushing" or "excited", instead of guessing the right category.

How it works

Tap a face to copy it. Tap the heart to save it to your favorites, which are stored in your own browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and nothing to install.

Our approach

We would rather have fewer, well-sorted faces than a huge pile of near-duplicates. New faces and collections are added over time, and each collection page explains what its faces are good for.

Get in touch

Found a face that does not display properly, or have an idea for a collection? Please contact us.
