How to copy and paste emoji

Emoji copy paste is simple here. Tap an emoji to copy it. Then paste it into your message with a long press on a phone, or Ctrl+V on Windows and Cmd+V on a Mac. You can also tap the heart on any card to save it to your favorites on this device.

Emoji vs kaomoji

Emoji are small pictures that come from the Unicode standard, and each company draws them in its own style. Kaomoji are Japanese-style faces made from text symbols, so they look the same everywhere. Many people use both. See what is kaomoji for the difference, or try the kaomoji generator.

Why emoji look different on each device

Apple, Google, Samsung, and others each design their own emoji, so the same emoji can look different between an iPhone and an Android phone. The meaning stays the same. Very new emoji may show as a box on old devices, which is why this site mostly uses well-supported emoji.

Tips for using emoji well

  • Use one or two emoji, not a long chain.
  • Match the feeling to the message, and add a word if the tone might be unclear.
  • Check slang meanings first, since emoji can mean different things to different people.
  • Keep emoji short in bios and names, where space is limited.

Questions

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap the emoji on this site. It is copied to your clipboard, and you can paste it into any app.

What is the difference between emoji and kaomoji?

Emoji are pictures from the Unicode standard. Kaomoji are text faces made from symbols, such as (˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶).

Why does an emoji show as a box?

Your device may be too old to include that emoji. Updating the system or using a more common emoji usually fixes it.

Do emoji look the same on every phone?

No. Each company draws its own style, so the look changes, but the meaning stays the same.

Can I save my favorite emoji?

Yes. Tap the heart on a card to save it on this device, and find it again on the homepage under My favorites.
