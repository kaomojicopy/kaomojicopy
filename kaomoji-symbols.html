Kaomoji symbols and emoticon characters

Every kaomoji is built from small symbols. This guide lists the most common emoticon symbols used in text faces, with their Unicode code points and official names, so you can see how a face is put together and why it sometimes breaks.

What are kaomoji symbols?

Kaomoji symbols are the text characters that make up a kaomoji. They come from many parts of Unicode, the standard that gives every character a code number. Some are letters from other alphabets, like the Greek ω. Others are shapes, katakana, or punctuation. Used together, they draw a face.

How a kaomoji is put together

Most faces follow the same pattern: an outline made of brackets, a pair of eyes, a mouth, and sometimes cheeks, arms, or decorations. For example, in (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ the づ marks are arms, ｡ marks are cheeks, ◕ marks are eyes, and ‿‿ is the smile. Changing one symbol changes the mood of the whole face.

Symbols and emoticons: what is the difference?

Symbols are single characters, like ♡ or ✿. Emoticons are faces built from several symbols. People also search for emoticons symbols or emoticon symbols, and both mean the characters used to build text faces. They are the parts, and a kaomoji is the finished face. You can use symbols alone as decoration, or combine them to make your own emoticon. See aesthetic kaomoji for examples.

How to type kaomoji symbols

The easiest way is to copy a whole face from this site. If you want to type a symbol yourself, you have a few options. In Microsoft Word on Windows, you can type the code, such as 2661, and press Alt and X to turn it into ♡. On Windows, the emoji panel (Windows key and period) has a kaomoji tab. On a Mac, the Character Viewer in the Edit menu lists symbols you can insert. Many phone keyboards also have a symbols page.

Why some kaomoji symbols show as boxes

A box means the font on your device does not include that character. Rare symbols, like some Kannada letters or combining marks, are the usual cause. Try another face, switch apps, or update your system. For more help, read how to copy and paste kaomoji.
