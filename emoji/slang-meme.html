Skull and fire emoji

The skull 💀 is often used for I am dead, meaning something is very funny. The skull and crossbones ☠️ can show danger or dark humor. The fire 🔥 means something is great, popular, or impressive. A dead emoji in slang is usually the skull 💀, and a cool emoji like 😎 works for confidence.

Cool, cold and hot

The face with sunglasses 😎 means cool and confident. The cold face 🥶 can mean freezing, or being stylish in slang. The hot face 🥵 can mean very warm, or someone is attractive.

Cap, clown and eyes

The billed cap 🧢 is used for cap, meaning a lie, and no cap means no lie. The clown face 🤡 calls something foolish. The eyes 👀 mean watching, or drama is coming.

Attitude emoji

The nail polish 💅 shows you are unbothered or sassy. The pinched fingers 🤌 can mean chef's kiss, or a gesture of disbelief. The hundred points 💯 shows full agreement.

What these slang emoji mean

Common meanings today:

  • 💀 skull: dead from laughing.
  • 🔥 fire: excellent, trendy, impressive.
  • 🧢 billed cap: a lie, as in cap.
  • 🤡 clown face: acting foolish.
  • 👀 eyes: watching or interested.
  • 💅 nail polish: unbothered and confident.
  • 💯 hundred points: agree completely.
