Surprised and shocked faces

The face with open mouth 😮 and the hushed face 😯 are gentle surprise. The astonished face 😲 is a bigger gasp. The flushed face 😳 shows shock or embarrassment, and the exploding head 🤯 shows a mind blown. The shock emoji 😲 and the surprised emoji 😮 are the two to start with. If you feel nervous, a nervous emoji like 😰 fits.

Scared and fearful faces

The fearful face 😨 and the face screaming in fear 😱 show fear. The anxious face with sweat 😰 shows worry, and the anguished face 😧 shows distress.

Dizzy and awkward faces

The dizzy face 😵 and the face with spiral eyes 😵‍💫 show confusion or feeling overwhelmed. The grimacing face 😬 and the zipper-mouth face 🤐 show an awkward moment.

What these shocked emoji mean

A short guide:

  • 😮 face with open mouth: mild surprise.
  • 😲 astonished face: big shock.
  • 😳 flushed face: shocked or embarrassed.
  • 🤯 exploding head: mind blown.
  • 😱 face screaming in fear: fear or big shock.
  • 😰 anxious face with sweat: worry.
  • 😬 grimacing face: an awkward moment.

Questions

What does the shocked emoji mean?

It shows surprise. The astonished face 😲 and the exploding head 🤯 are the strongest shocked emoji.

What does the flushed face mean?

😳 means shock or embarrassment, and sometimes shy feelings.

What is the scared emoji?

The fearful face 😨 and the face screaming in fear 😱 are the main scared emoji.

What does the exploding head mean?

🤯 means your mind is blown, by news or an idea.

Which emoji is best for a surprise party?

Try 🥳 or 😲, depending on whether you are the guest or the guest of honor.
