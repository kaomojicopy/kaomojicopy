Crying emoji and sob emoji

The crying face 😢 shows a single tear and plain sadness. The loudly crying face 😭 shows heavy tears and sobbing, but many people also use it to mean laughing so hard they are crying, or being overwhelmed by something sweet or funny. The sob emoji can therefore be sad or playful, so read the context. People also search for the cry emoji, and the official name for it is simply crying face 😢. The sobbing emoji 😭 is its louder cousin.

Sad emoji for different kinds of sadness

Not every sad emoji is the same. The disappointed face 😞 and pensive face 😔 show quiet sadness. The worried face 😟 and anxious face with sweat 😰 show nerves. The pleading face 🥺 asks for something, or shows that you are touched. Pick the one that matches how strong the feeling is. These sad emojis, along with a sadness emoji like 😞, cover quiet disappointment as well as strong grief.

Bittersweet and relieved faces

The smiling face with tear 🥲 mixes a smile and a tear. People use it for pride, relief, and bittersweet moments. The sad but relieved face 😥 shows relief after stress.

Crying emoji or sad kaomoji?

If you like text faces, sad kaomoji have tears, sighs, and sobbing in text form. Emoji and kaomoji work well together.

What these crying and sad emoji mean

A short guide:

  • 😢 crying face: sadness, a single tear.
  • 😭 loudly crying face: heavy crying, or laughing and feeling overwhelmed.
  • 🥲 smiling face with tear: pride, relief, or bittersweet feelings.
  • 🥺 pleading face: begging, or being touched.
  • 😞 disappointed face: quiet disappointment.
  • 😰 anxious face with sweat: worry and nerves.
  • 💔 broken heart: heartbreak.

Questions

What does the crying emoji mean?

It usually means sadness, but the loudly crying face is also used for laughing hard or feeling overwhelmed.

What is the difference between 😢 and 😭?

😢 shows one tear and quiet sadness. 😭 shows heavy crying, and in slang it can also mean something is very funny or touching.

What does the smiling face with a tear mean?

It means a mix of happy and sad, such as pride, relief, or a bittersweet memory.

Which emoji should I use to say sorry?

A gentle face like the pleading face 🥺 or the pensive face 😔, paired with a few words, works best.

Is there a sad emoji for a cat?

Yes. The crying cat face 😿 is a sad cat emoji.
