Cat face emoji and their moods

The cat face 🐱 is the simple one. The grinning cat 😺 and the grinning cat with smiling eyes 😸 show happiness. The cat with tears of joy 😹 is laughing, and the smiling cat with heart-eyes 😻 is in love. The cat with wry smile 😼 is smug, the kissing cat 😽 sends a kiss, and the weary cat 🙀 is shocked.

Sad and grumpy cats

The crying cat 😿 is a sad cat emoji, and the pouting cat 😾 is angry or grumpy. They are useful for jokes about bad days or hungry pets.

Paws, black cats and big cats

The paw prints 🐾 add a cat or pet touch to any message. The black cat 🐈‍⬛ is used for cute or spooky posts. 🦁, 🐯, and 🐆 are the big cats, good for strong or playful moods.

What these cat emoji mean

A quick guide:

  • 🐱 cat face: a cute cat, or a general pet.
  • 😺 grinning cat: happy and pleased.
  • 😹 cat with tears of joy: laughing hard.
  • 😻 smiling cat with heart-eyes: in love.
  • 😼 cat with wry smile: smug or sneaky.
  • 😿 crying cat: sad.
  • 🐾 paw prints: pets, tracks, and playful footsteps.
