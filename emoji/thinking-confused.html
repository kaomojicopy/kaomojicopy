Thinking face and friends

The thinking face 🤔 means you are wondering or doubting. The face with monocle 🧐 looks closely, as if checking details. The face with raised eyebrow 🤨 shows suspicion or disbelief. Use the think emoji 🤔 when you wonder about something. A confusing emoji like 😕 fits when something does not make sense.

Confused and uncertain faces

The confused face 😕 and the worried face 😟 show confusion. The neutral face 😐, the expressionless face 😑, and the face without mouth 😶 show no reaction, which can be funny or awkward.

Shrugging emoji

The person shrugging 🤷 means I do not know. If you like the text version, copy the classic from our shrug emoticons page.

What these thinking emoji mean

A short guide:

  • 🤔 thinking face: wondering or doubting.
  • 🧐 face with monocle: examining something closely.
  • 🤨 face with raised eyebrow: suspicion.
  • 😕 confused face: not sure.
  • 🤷 person shrugging: I do not know.
  • 🤫 shushing face: keep it quiet.
  • 💭 thought balloon: thinking or dreaming.
