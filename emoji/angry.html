Angry face emoji

The angry face 😠 shows plain anger. The enraged face 😡 is red with rage and is the strongest. The face with symbols on mouth 🤬 shows swearing in a comic way. The angry face with horns 👿 is angry in a playful, devilish way.

Annoyed and unamused emoji

For smaller annoyance, use the unamused face 😒, the face with rolling eyes 🙄, or the expressionless face 😑. The grimacing face 😬 shows an awkward, tense moment.

Steam and frustration

The face with steam from nose 😤 shows frustration, and many people also use it for determination. The anger symbol 💢 adds a veins-popping effect to any message. If something is annoying, an annoying emoji like 🙄 shows it without needing many words.

What these angry emoji mean

A short guide:

  • 😠 angry face: plain anger.
  • 😡 enraged face: strong rage.
  • 🤬 face with symbols on mouth: furious, swearing.
  • 😤 face with steam from nose: frustration or determination.
  • 😒 unamused face: not impressed.
  • 🙄 face with rolling eyes: annoyance or disbelief.
  • 💢 anger symbol: a burst of anger.

Questions

What does the angry emoji mean?

It shows anger. The red enraged face 😡 is the strongest version.

What does 😤 mean?

It can mean frustration, but it is also used to show determination or pride.

Which emoji shows annoyance?

The unamused face 😒 and the face with rolling eyes 🙄 show mild annoyance.

Is the angry face with horns the same as the devil?

The angry face with horns 👿 is a playful angry face. The smiling face with horns 😈 is mischievous.

Can angry emoji sound rude?

Yes, they can. Pair them with a light word, or choose a milder face like 😒, if you want to stay friendly.
