Heart emoji colors and what they mean

The red heart ❤️ is the classic sign of love, and the safest choice for partners, family, and close friends. Orange 🧡, yellow 💛, and green 💚 hearts are often used for friendship, happiness, and care. Blue 💙 and purple 💜 hearts feel calm and loyal. The black 🖤 heart is used for dark humor or a stylish look, and the white 🤍 heart for soft, pure feelings. Colors can mean different things in different communities, such as fan groups, so match the meaning to the people you are talking to. If you are looking for emojis with hearts, a hearts emoji in a bright color is the simplest way to show you care.

Kisses, heart eyes and sparkling hearts

Faces add emotion to a heart. 😍 heart eyes shows you adore something, 🥰 smiling face with hearts feels warm and affectionate, and 😘 face blowing a kiss is a friendly or romantic goodbye. Sparkling hearts like 💖 and growing hearts like 💗 suit sweet, excited messages, while 💔 broken heart shows heartbreak or sadness.

What these love emoji mean

A quick guide to the most popular ones:

  • ❤️ red heart: love, affection, and thanks.
  • 💕 two hearts: affection and happiness, often for couples or friends.
  • 💞 revolving hearts: a warm, flowing kind of love.
  • 💘 heart with arrow: falling in love, or a crush.
  • 💝 heart with ribbon: a gift of love, or deep appreciation.
  • ❤️‍🔥 heart on fire: passion and strong feeling.
  • 💔 broken heart: sadness, heartbreak, or disappointment.

Questions

What does the red heart emoji mean?

It means love and affection. People also use it to say thank you or to show support.

What do the different colored hearts mean?

Red is romantic love, while other colors are often used for friendship, care, or a favorite team. Meanings can vary by community.

Which heart emoji is best for a crush?

Heart eyes 😍 and the heart with arrow 💘 are popular for a crush. Keep it light if you are not sure how the other person feels.

Why do emoji look different on iPhone and Android?

Each company draws its own version of every emoji. The meaning stays the same, but the style can change.

Can I paste heart emoji into any app?

Yes, almost every chat, social, and writing app accepts emoji. Very old devices may show a box for newer ones.
