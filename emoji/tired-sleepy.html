Sleeping emoji

The sleeping face 😴 shows someone fast asleep, and 💤 adds zzz. The sleepy face 😪 has a bubble of snot and shows drowsiness. 🛌 shows a person in bed, a good choice for good night messages. The tiredness emoji most people use is the weary face 😩, and a sleep emoji like 😴 or 💤 shows actual sleep.

Tired and weary emoji

The yawning face 🥱 shows boredom or sleepiness. The tired face 😫 and the weary face 😩 show exhaustion and frustration. The face exhaling 😮‍💨 shows a big sigh, often from relief or fatigue.

Cozy extras

A warm drink like ☕ or 🍵 suits slow mornings. 🌙 is a classic for good night messages.

What these tired emoji mean

A short guide:

  • 😴 sleeping face: fast asleep.
  • 💤 zzz: sleeping, or boredom.
  • 🥱 yawning face: sleepy or bored.
  • 😪 sleepy face: drowsy.
  • 😫 tired face: worn out and frustrated.
  • 😮‍💨 face exhaling: a big sigh.
  • 🛌 person in bed: time to sleep.

Questions

What is the tired emoji?

The tired face 😫 and the weary face 😩 are the main tired emoji. The yawning face 🥱 shows sleepiness.

What does 💤 mean?

It stands for sleeping, and it is also used when something is boring.

Which emoji should I use for good night?

Try 😴, 🌙, or 🛌, with a short good night message.

What does the face exhaling mean?

It shows a big sigh, for relief, tiredness, or giving up.

Is there a cozy emoji for tea or coffee?

Yes. Use ☕ for coffee or 🍵 for tea.
