Shock face, surprise and gasps

A shock face has round eyes and a small round mouth, like (°o°) or (°ロ°). It shows a quick gasp. Add a sparkle or a heart next to it for happy surprise.

Nervous, scared and confused faces

Sweat marks and wobbly mouths show nerves, like (°o°;). A confused face, like (・・;), says wait, what? Scared faces add more shaking, such as ((((；ﾟДﾟ)))).

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our shocked, surprised and scared emoji page.

What these surprised kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (°o°): degree-sign eyes and an o mouth show shock.
  • (⊙_⊙): ⊙ eyes are wide open.
  • (°o°;): the semicolon is a sweat drop for nerves.
  • (・・;): small dot eyes and a sweat mark show confusion.
  • ((((；ﾟДﾟ)))): the extra brackets show shaking in fear.

Questions

Which kaomoji shows shock?

Faces with big round eyes, like (⊙_⊙), and a small open mouth read as shocked in almost any chat.

Can a surprised face also mean happy surprise?

Yes. Add a sparkle or a heart next to it, or a few words like no way, to show it is good news.

What does a nervous face look like?

It usually has wide eyes plus a sweat mark, written with a semicolon, like (°o°;).

Do these work in comments and captions?

Yes. They are plain text, so they work anywhere you can type.
