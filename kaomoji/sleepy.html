Sleepy kaomoji

Sleepy kaomoji are for naps, late nights, and mornings that start too early. Closed eyes and little zzz marks do most of the work, so these faces are quick to read.

They are handy for saying good night, telling friends you are logging off, or showing you are tired. Tap a face to copy it and paste it anywhere.

Good night and nap faces

Add a sleepy face after a short line like good night or see you tomorrow. Faces such as (｡-ω-)zzz and (-.-)Zzz… show dozing off, while calmer faces show a cozy rest.

Cozy, tired and slow-morning moods

Sleepy does not always mean sleeping. A cozy face, such as the tea break, fits a slow morning, and a tired sigh fits a long day. For gentle goodbyes, try greeting kaomoji.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our tired and sleeping emoji page.

Questions

How do I write a good night message with a kaomoji?

Add a sleepy face after a short line like good night or see you tomorrow. Keeping it simple works best.

What does zzz mean in a message?

It is a common way to show someone sleeping, borrowed from comics.

Can I use these on Discord or WhatsApp?

Yes. They are plain text, so they paste into both.

Is there a face for being tired but happy?

Yes. Look for the cozy faces with a small smile and closed eyes.
