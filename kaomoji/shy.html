Blush faces and blushy faces

Blush lines are drawn with slashes or marks beside the eyes, as in (*/∇＼*) and (ﾉ∀`〃). The more lines, the stronger the blush.

Hiding faces

Some shy kaomoji cover the face with a hand, like (／ω＼). They are good for compliments you do not know how to answer. For a sweeter look, see cute kaomoji.

What these shy kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (*/∇＼*): the / and ＼ marks are blush lines.
  • (ﾉ∀`〃): 〃 marks are blush on the cheeks.
  • (／ω＼): the / and ＼ marks are hands hiding the face.
  • (⸝⸝>﹏<⸝⸝): ⸝⸝ are blush marks around squeezed-shut eyes.
  • (〃▽〃): 〃 on each side are blush marks.

Questions

What do the slashes in a shy kaomoji mean?

They stand for blush lines on the cheeks, a style that comes from manga.

When should I use a shy kaomoji?

After a compliment, a confession, or an awkward moment. It tells the other person you are flustered, not upset.

Can I use shy faces for flirting?

Yes, they are often used that way. Keep it light and match the other person's tone.

Is a blush face the same as a blushy face?

They mean the same thing. Different people use different words for a face with blush marks.
