Questions

What do the slashes in a shy kaomoji mean?

They stand for blush lines on the cheeks, a style that comes from manga.

When should I use a shy kaomoji?

After a compliment, a confession, or an awkward moment. It tells the other person you are flustered, not upset.

Can I use shy faces for flirting?

Yes, they are often used that way. Keep it light and match the other person's tone.
