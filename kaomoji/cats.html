How cat emoticons are drawn

Most cat kaomoji put a small face in the middle and mark the ears and whiskers on each side, as in (=^･ω･^=). Some add a tail or paws, like ᓚ₍ ^. .^₎ and ฅ•ω•ฅ.

Happy, sleepy and sad cat faces

Cats show mood just like people. A happy cat smiles, a sleepy cat closes its eyes, and a sad cat face droops, like (=;ェ;=). Use the sad cat when you want to be dramatic in a funny way.

Using cat kaomoji in posts

Cat faces work well in captions, bios, and comments on pet photos. If you like animal faces, also see bear kaomoji and bunny kaomoji.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our cat emoji page.

What these cat kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (=^･ω･^=): ^ marks are ears and = marks are whiskers around a little face.
  • ᓚ₍ ^. .^₎: the ᓚ is often a tail, and ^ marks are cat ears.
  • (=;ェ;=): ; eyes are tears and ェ is a small mouth on a sad cat.
  • ฅ•ω•ฅ: the ฅ characters are paws raised on each side.
  • ≽^•⩊•^≼: ≽ and ≼ are pointed cat ears.

Questions

How are cat kaomoji drawn?

Most are built from plain characters: a face in the middle, with ^ and = marks for ears and whiskers.

Can I use cat kaomoji in usernames?

Many sites allow special characters in display names, but rules differ. Check the site before you save.

Is there a sleepy or grumpy cat?

Yes, both are on this page. Look for the napping cat and the grumpy cat.

What is a sad cat face?

A cat kaomoji with drooping eyes or tears, used to show a dramatic sad mood.
