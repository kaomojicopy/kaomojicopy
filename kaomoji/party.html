Questions

Which kaomoji is best for a birthday message?

Pick a face with raised arms or a balloon, then add the person's name and a short wish.

Can I use emoji together with kaomoji?

Yes. A few faces here already include a balloon or cake. Keep the message simple so it stays easy to read.

Do celebration kaomoji work in group chats?

Yes. They are plain text, so they paste into group chats, though fonts can change how a face looks, so it may not appear identical to everyone.
