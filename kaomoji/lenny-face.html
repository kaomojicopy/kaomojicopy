Lenny face

The lenny face ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°) is a cheeky, knowing look that spread through internet forums in the early 2010s. It is usually used as a joke or a playful hint.

How the lenny face is built

The lenny face uses degree signs as eyes and the letter ʖ as a mouth. Small combining marks above the eyes bend over the face, giving it a raised-eyebrow look. These marks are what make the face tricky to type and easy to break.

Lenny face variants

Variants change the eyes or add decoration: a sad lenny, a wink lenny, a happy lenny, a wide-eyed lenny, and even a lenny bear. Pick the one that fits your joke.

When to use the lenny face

Lenny faces suit light, joking moments:

  • As a playful hint between friends
  • After a cheeky joke
  • As a reaction to something silly or suspicious
  • Because the tone can read as suggestive, avoid it in formal or work chats.

What these lenny faces mean

How the variants differ:

  • ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°): the classic. Degree-sign eyes and the ʖ mouth give a knowing smirk.
  • ( ͡° ʖ̯ ͡°): a flatter mouth makes the sad lenny.
  • ( ͡~ ͜ʖ ͡°): one eye is a tilde, so the lenny winks.
  • ( ͡ᵔ ͜ʖ ͡ᵔ ): curved eyes make a happy, smiling lenny.
  • ( ͡° ᴥ ͡°): the ᴥ mouth gives a bear-like lenny.

Questions

What is the lenny face?

The lenny face is a meme face, ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°), used for a cheeky or knowing look. It became popular on internet forums in the early 2010s.

Why does the lenny face look broken?

It uses combining marks that some apps draw badly. If it looks off, try another variant or a different app.

How do I type the lenny face?

Copy it from this page. It uses combining characters that are hard to type by hand.

Is the lenny face rude?

It is meant as cheeky, but some people read it as suggestive. Use it with friends and avoid it in formal chats.

Are there other lenny faces?

Yes. Many variants change the eyes or add objects, such as the sad, wink, and happy ones on this page.
