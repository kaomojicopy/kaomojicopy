Angry face vs annoyed face

An angry face has slanted eyes and a hard mouth, like (｀へ´) or (｀Δ´)！. An annoyed face is quieter: a flat stare or a sideways look, like (¬_¬). Pick annoyed for small irritations and angry for bigger ones. A scowl face like (`⌓´) glares with a tight frown.

Pouty and pouting faces

A pouty face sulks instead of shouting. It is a playful way to complain, ask for attention, or say you are not happy that plans changed. Look for faces like (•ˋ _ ˊ•) and (¬з¬). For a softer version, try cute kaomoji.

What these angry kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (｀へ´): ｀ and ´ are slanted eyebrows, and へ is a tight frown.
  • (｀Δ´)！: the Δ mouth is clenched, and ！ adds shouting.
  • (•ˋ _ ˊ•): ˋ and ˊ tilt the eyebrows for a pouting look.
  • (`⌓´): ⌓ is a flat, scowling mouth.
  • (◣_◢): the ◣ and ◢ eyes are angular, a dark stare.

Questions

Can I use angry faces without sounding rude?

Yes, if you pair them with a joking line. A face on its own can sound sharper than you mean it.

What is a pouty face?

It is a face with a downturned or pursed mouth that shows sulking in a playful way.

Where is the table flip kaomoji?

It has its own page, table flip kaomoji, together with the matching face that puts the table back.

Why do some faces show empty boxes?

Your device may not have a font for one of the symbols. Try another face, or update your system.
