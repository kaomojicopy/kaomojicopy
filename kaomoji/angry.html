Questions

What is the table flip kaomoji?

It is (╯°□°）╯︵ ┻━┻, a face that throws a table in frustration. The matching ┬─┬ノ( º _ ºノ) puts the table back.

Can I use angry faces without sounding rude?

Yes, if you pair them with a joking line. A face on its own can sound sharper than you mean it.

Why do some faces show empty boxes?

Your device may not have a font for one of the symbols. Try another face, or update your system.
