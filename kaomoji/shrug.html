Shrug emoticon

How the shrug emoticon is built

In ¯\_(ツ)_/¯, the katakana ツ looks like a smiling face. The characters on each side of the face form the arms and shoulders, and the underscores are the hands. Put together, the face looks like someone shrugging.

Shrug emoji and shrugging emoji

If you are searching for a shrug emoji, there is a picture version (🤷), and it changes look from one device to another. The text shrug is a kaomoji, so it looks the same everywhere. You can also use both in the same message.

Shrugging faces for different moods

A calm shrug like ┐( ˘_˘)┌ says why not. A nervous shrug like ┐(‘～`；)┌ adds a sweat mark. A helpless shrug like ╮(╯_╰)╭ shows you cannot fix it. A happy shrug like ╮(︶▽︶)╭ says it is fine either way.

Why the shrug loses its arm

In Discord, Slack, and other apps that use markdown, a backslash before an underscore can be read as formatting, which hides the arm. Type a second backslash, or paste the face inside a code block, to keep it whole. Or use a version without a backslash, like ┐(´д｀)┌.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our thinking and confused emoji page.

What these shrugging faces mean

A few of the faces on this page, explained:

  • ¯\_(ツ)_/¯: the classic shrug. The face in the middle smiles a little, so it means whatever, with no worry.
  • ┐(´д｀)┌: the ┐ and ┌ marks are shoulders raised in a shrug, and the д makes a sighing mouth.
  • ╮(╯_╰)╭: the ╮ and ╭ marks are lifted shoulders, and the ╯ and ╰ marks are tired eyes, a helpless shrug.
  • ヽ(ー_ー )ノ: raised arms with flat eyes show a relaxed, whatever shrug.
  • ¯\_(⊙︿⊙)_/¯: wide eyes and a wobbly mouth show a worried shrug.

Questions

What is the shrug emoticon?

It is the text face ¯\_(ツ)_/¯, used to say I do not know or whatever.

How do I type the shrug emoticon?

Copy it from this page. Typing it by hand is difficult because of the backslash and the katakana character.

Is there a shrug emoji?

Yes. The person shrugging emoji (🤷) is a picture. The text shrug is a kaomoji and looks the same on every device.

Why does the shrug look broken on Discord?

Discord reads a backslash before an underscore as formatting. Add a second backslash, or paste the shrug inside a code block.

What does the shrug mean?

It means I do not know, I do not mind, or there is nothing to be done. The tone depends on the face you choose.
