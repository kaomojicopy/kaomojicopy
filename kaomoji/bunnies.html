Questions

How do bunny kaomoji get their ears?

Most place a special symbol on each side of the face so it looks like ears or little paws.

Are bunny kaomoji good for Easter?

Yes, they are popular for spring and Easter greetings. Add a short message and you are done.

Why do some bunny faces look broken on my device?

They use less common symbols that some fonts do not include. Try another bunny, or update your device fonts.
