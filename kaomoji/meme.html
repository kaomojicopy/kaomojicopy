Meme faces

Meme faces are the text reactions that spread through forums, chats, and comment sections: the facepalm, the deadpan stare, the derp face, and the sus look. They work best as quick reactions, one face with no extra words.

Facepalm: for disbelief and embarrassment

A facepalm face shows a hand covering the face. The face (－‸ლ) uses ლ as the hand, and (ーー;) shows the same mood with a sweat mark. People use a facepalm after a silly mistake, a bad joke, or something they cannot believe. If you are searching for a facepalm emoji, there is a picture version too (🤦), but the text facepalm is a kaomoji and looks the same in every app.

Deadpan, straight face and stone face

Deadpan faces show no expression at all. A straight face like (・_・), a flat face like (－_－), a stone face like (◔_◔), and a stoic face like ( ´_ゝ`) all say I am not reacting. They are funny after something silly, because the lack of reaction is the joke.

Derp face and duck face

The derp face ( ﾟдﾟ) is a blank, open-mouthed stare. The duck face (*￣з￣) puckers its lips. Both are silly faces for jokes between friends.

Sus faces and deal with it

A sus face has wide, side-eyed eyes, like (⊙_◎) or (ʘ‿ʘ), and says I do not trust this. The deal with it face (⌐■_■) wears sunglasses to show cool confidence.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our slang and meme emoji page.

What these meme faces mean

Here is how a few of them are put together:

  • (－‸ლ): the ლ at the end is a hand over the face, a facepalm.
  • (・_・): two dots for eyes and a dot for a mouth, so there is no expression.
  • ( ﾟдﾟ): the Cyrillic д makes a wide-open mouth, giving a shocked or blank stare.
  • (⌐■_■): the black squares are sunglasses, and ⌐ is the frame.
  • ಠ‿ಠ: the Kannada letter ಠ works as a wide eye, and ‿ adds a suspicious smile.

Questions

What is a facepalm kaomoji?

It is a text face that shows a hand over the face, used for disbelief or embarrassment.

What does the sus face mean?

A sus face is a suspicious look with wide, sideways eyes. It means something seems wrong or fishy.

How do I use the deal with it face?

Send it after a confident or cheeky statement. The sunglasses make it feel like a mic drop.

Where are the shrug and table flip?

They have their own pages: shrug emoticons and table flip kaomoji.

Why do some meme faces show boxes?

Some use rare characters that older fonts miss. Try another face, or update your system.
