Sassy faces are best for jokes between friends. Pair them with a light line, and avoid them in serious or formal chats. For stronger reactions, see shrug emoticons, lenny faces, and meme faces.

Questions

What is a smug face kaomoji?

It is a text face with a confident or teasing look, often with narrowed eyes and a slight smile.

Are sassy faces rude?

Not usually, but tone depends on the situation. With friends they read as playful. In a work chat, a milder face is safer.

What is the difference between a smirk and a smug face?

A smirk is a quick, cheeky grin. A smug face looks more pleased with itself and lasts longer in tone.

Where is the shrug kaomoji?
