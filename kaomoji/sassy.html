Questions

What is the shrug kaomoji?

It is ¯\_(ツ)_/¯, a classic face that means I do not know or whatever. You can copy it from this page.

Why does the shrug sometimes lose its arm?

Some apps treat a backslash before an underscore as formatting and hide the backslash. Typing a second backslash, or pasting it inside a code block, usually fixes it.

Are sassy faces rude?

Not usually, but tone depends on the situation. With friends they read as playful. In a work chat, a milder face is safer.
