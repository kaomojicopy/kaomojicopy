Hugs and kisses

Faces with arms, like (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ, look like a hug in almost any chat. Faces with a small mouth and a ♡ beside them, like (˘³˘)♥, read as a kiss. Add a short line such as miss you or good night to make the meaning clear.

Heart emoticons, heart kaomoji and heart eyes

Heart kaomoji and heart emoticons use symbols such as ♡, ♥, and ❤ next to a face, or as the eyes of the face itself. Heart eyes show that you adore something, whether that is a person, a pet, or a snack. Pick a soft face for sweet messages and a bigger one for excitement.

Love kaomoji for friends and family

Love is not only for couples. Many people send a hug to a friend who had a rough day, a heart to a family member, or a blushing face after a kind compliment. Use a lighter face for friends and save the closest ones for people you know well. For a shyer tone, see blushing kaomoji.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our love and heart emoji page.

What these love kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ: づ at each end are arms reaching for a hug.
  • (♡ᴗ♡): ♡ replaces the eyes, so the face is lovestruck.
  • (❤ω❤): ❤ eyes and an ω mouth give a soft heart-eyes look.
  • (˘³˘)♥: the ³ is pursed lips blowing a kiss, with ♥ beside it.
  • (⊃｡•́‿•̀｡)⊃: ⊃ marks are open arms asking for a hug.

Questions

Which kaomoji are best for sending a hug?

Faces with arms, such as (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ, look like a hug in most chats. Try a few and see which one fits your style.

What is a love emoticon?

A love emoticon is a text face or symbol that shows affection, usually with a heart, a kiss, or heart eyes.

Can I use love kaomoji with friends and family?

Yes. Hearts and hugs are not only for couples. Many people use them to cheer up a friend, thank a family member, or say well done.

How do I type a heart in a kaomoji?

The easiest way is to copy the whole face from this page. Heart symbols such as ♡ and ♥ are normal text characters, so they paste like any other letter.

Why does a kaomoji look different on my phone?

Each device draws special symbols with its own fonts, so spacing and shapes can differ a little. The text itself stays the same when you paste it.
