Questions

How do I copy a happy kaomoji?

Tap the face or the Copy button. It goes to your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

Do happy kaomoji work on every phone?

Yes. They are plain text characters, so they work in Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram, and most other apps.

Why do the sparkles look different on my device?

Each device draws special symbols with its own fonts, so spacing and shapes can differ slightly. The text you paste is the same.
