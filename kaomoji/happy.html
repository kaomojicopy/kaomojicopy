Smiley faces and calm smiles

Classic smiley kaomoji such as (^_^) and (^‿^) are the text version of a smiley face. They are low-key and work in almost any chat, including friendly work messages.

Excited and celebrating kaomoji

For big news, pick a face with raised arms or sparkles, like ＼(＾▽＾)／ or (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧. They suit birthdays, results, and good news. You will find more on the celebration kaomoji page.

Laughing and joyful faces

Faces with wide open mouths and squeezed eyes, like (≧▽≦) and o(≧▽≦)o, show laughter or joy. Pair one with a short line such as that made my day.

Happy kaomoji or happy emoji?

Emoji are tiny pictures that change look from one app to another. Happy kaomoji are plain text, so they paste the same everywhere and can be longer and more expressive. Many people use both. For cuter styles, try cute kaomoji.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our happy, smiling and laughing emoji page.

What these happy kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (＾▽＾): ＾ eyes curve upward and ▽ is an open mouth, a big smile.
  • ＼(＾▽＾)／: the ＼ and ／ marks are raised arms for cheering.
  • (≧▽≦): ≧ and ≦ squeeze the eyes shut with joy, like a big laugh.
  • (ᵔ◡ᵔ): ᵔ eyes and a ◡ smile make a calm smiley face.
  • (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧: ヮ is an open grin, ﾉ is a raised arm, and *:･ﾟ✧ sprinkles sparkles.

Questions

What is a happy kaomoji?

It is a text face that shows joy, such as a smile, raised arms, or bright eyes. It is built from letters and symbols instead of a picture.

How do I copy a happy kaomoji?

Tap the face or the Copy button. It goes to your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

What is the best kaomoji for good news?

Faces with raised arms or sparkles work best, such as the cheering and excited faces on this page.

Do these faces work on every phone?

Yes. They are plain text characters, so they work in Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram, and most other apps.

Are happy kaomoji the same as smiley faces?

A smiley face is one kind of happy face. Happy kaomoji include smileys but also cover excitement, laughter, and celebration.
