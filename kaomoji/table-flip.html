How the table flip is built

In (╯°□°）╯︵ ┻━┻, the face (°□°) has round eyes and a square mouth. The ╯ is a raised arm, ︵ is the flying motion, and ┻━┻ is the table, upside down. The table starts as ┬─┬ and ends up flipped.

Putting the table back

The calm partner ┬─┬ノ( º _ ºノ) shows someone setting the table upright again. People send it after a table flip to say they have cooled down, or to reply to a friend who flipped one.

Angry, sad and double table flips

Table flipping comes in many moods, so if you are searching for flipping tables, you will find a face for each. Variants change the mood: an angry flip, a shocked flip, a sad flip, and a double table flip for when one is not enough. See angry face kaomoji for other mad faces.

What these table flips mean

How a few flips differ:

  • (╯°□°）╯︵ ┻━┻: the classic. Surprised eyes and a raised arm launch the table.
  • (ノಠ益ಠ)ノ彡┻━┻: ಠ益ಠ is a furious face, and 彡 shows the table flying.
  • (┛◉Д◉)┛彡┻━┻: wide ◉ eyes and a Д mouth show shock while flipping.
  • (ﾉಥ益ಥ）ﾉ ┻━┻: ಥ eyes are tears, so this is a sad, crying flip.
  • ┬─┬ノ( º _ ºノ): the calm reset, putting the table back.
