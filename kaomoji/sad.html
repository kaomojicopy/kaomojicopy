Questions

When is it okay to use a sad kaomoji?

Whenever you want to show feeling in a casual chat. For serious news, a few honest words usually say more than a face.

What is the difference between crying and sad faces?

Crying faces usually show tears with marks like ; or T, while sad faces use droopy eyes and a small frown. Pick the one that matches your mood.

How do I comfort a friend with a kaomoji?

Look for faces with a hug or soft eyes on the sad and love pages, and add one short kind line next to it.
