Hello and good morning faces

A waving face with a smile works as hello at any time of day. Add the person's name or a short good morning to make it personal.

Goodbye and good night

For the end of a chat, use a waving face or a sleepy one. You can find more on the sleepy kaomoji page.

What these greeting kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (＾▽＾)ノ: the ノ is a raised hand, a wave.
  • (´-ω-`)ﾉ゛: a sleepy face waving good night.
  • (^_^)/: the / is a raised arm saying goodbye.
  • ヾ(･ω･*)ﾉ: raised hands on both sides, a bright hello.

Questions

What does the ノ at the end of a face mean?

It looks like a raised hand, so the face appears to wave.

Is there a kaomoji for good night?

Yes. Check the good night face on this page, or the sleepy page for more.

Can I use greeting kaomoji at work?

In a friendly team chat, yes. For formal emails, plain words are safer.
