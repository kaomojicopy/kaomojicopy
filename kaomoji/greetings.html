Questions

What does the ノ at the end of a face mean?

It looks like a raised hand, so the face appears to wave.

Is there a kaomoji for good night?

Yes. Check the good night face on this page, or the sleepy page for more.

Can I use greeting kaomoji at work?

In a friendly team chat, yes. For formal emails, plain words are safer.
