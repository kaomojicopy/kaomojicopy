Star emoticons and star kaomoji

Star kaomoji use ★, ☆, and ✩ as eyes or as decoration, like (★ω★) and ⋆｡°✩. They look good at the end of a sentence or around a short phrase.

Sparkles and glitter

Kaomoji sparkles, ✧･ﾟ: *✧･ﾟ:* , add a magical feel. Try them before or after a caption to make it stand out.

Flower and ribbon emoticons

Flower emoticons use ✿ and ❀ for a spring or cottage look. Ribbon emoticons use a ribbon emoji beside a cute face. For simple faces without decoration, see cute kaomoji.

Prefer emoji?

Kaomoji are text, and emoji are pictures. If you want the picture version, see our cute and aesthetic emoji page.

What these aesthetic kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • (★ω★): ★ eyes show star-struck wonder.
  • ✧･ﾟ: *✧･ﾟ:*: sparkles only, used as decoration.
  • ✿◕ ‿ ◕✿: ✿ flowers frame a sweet face.
  • (｡•ᴗ•｡)🎀: a ribbon emoji adds a bow.
  • ୨♡୧: ୨ and ୧ frame a heart, like a tiny wreath.

Questions

What are aesthetic kaomoji?

They are kaomoji with extra decoration, like stars, flowers, and sparkles, made to look soft and stylish.

How do I use star emoticons in a bio?

Copy the face and paste it at the end of a line. Keep it short so the symbols do not push your text out of view.

Do sparkles and stars work on every device?

Most do, but a few small symbols may show as boxes on older devices. Try another one if that happens.

Can I use aesthetic kaomoji on TikTok and Instagram?

Yes. They are text, so you can use them in bios, captions, and comments.
