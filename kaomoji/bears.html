Questions

What makes a bear kaomoji look like a bear?

Usually a round nose symbol in the middle and curved marks on each side that look like ears.

Can I use a bear kaomoji to send a hug?

Yes. Look for the bear with open arms, or add a short hug message next to any bear.

Do bear kaomoji work in every app?

Almost always. The symbols are standard text, but a few older devices may show boxes for some of them.
