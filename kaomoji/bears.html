Why bear faces feel so friendly

The round nose and soft eyes make bears read as gentle. A happy bear, a sleepy bear, and a waving bear cover most everyday moods.

Bear hugs and greetings

Faces with raised arms work as a hug, like ʕつ•ᴥ•ʔつ. For hello and goodbye faces, see greeting kaomoji, or try cat kaomoji for more animals.

What these bear kaomoji mean

Here is how a few of the faces on this page are put together:

  • ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ: ʕ and ʔ are the round ears, • are eyes, and ᴥ is the nose.
  • ʕつ•ᴥ•ʔつ: the つ marks are arms reaching out for a hug.
  • ʕ ᵔᴥᵔ ʔ: ᵔ eyes give the bear a happy look.
  • ʕ-ᴥ-ʔ: dash eyes show a sleepy bear.

Questions

What makes a bear kaomoji look like a bear?

Usually a round nose symbol in the middle and curved marks on each side that look like ears.

Can I use a bear kaomoji to send a hug?

Yes. Look for the bear with open arms, or add a short hug message next to any bear.

Do bear kaomoji work in every app?

Almost always. The symbols are standard text, but a few older devices may show boxes for some of them.
