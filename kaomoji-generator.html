How to use the kaomoji generator

  • Pick a frame, eyes, and a mouth. The face updates as you choose.
  • Choose None in any group to leave that part out, for a simpler face.
  • Add cheeks, arms, or a decoration if you want more personality. Remove decoration clears all three at once.
  • Press Randomize parts to shuffle everything, or Clear all to start from a blank face.
  • Tap Copy to copy the face, or the heart to save it to your favorites.

How the parts fit together

The generator puts the pieces in a fixed order: arms, frame, cheeks, eyes, mouth, eyes again, cheeks again, the closing frame, arms, and finally the decoration. That is the same pattern most kaomoji follow. To learn what each symbol does, read our list of kaomoji symbols.

Tips for a good custom kaomoji

  • Match the mood: round eyes and a small mouth look cute, while ಠ eyes and a flat mouth look stern.
  • Keep it short. One decoration is usually enough.
  • Test the face in your app, since some symbols may look different there.
  • Not sure where to start? Browse cute kaomoji, happy kaomoji, or aesthetic kaomoji and change one part.

Random kaomoji from the library

The Random kaomoji button picks a ready-made face from our library, so you can discover styles you would not search for. Press it as many times as you like and copy the ones you enjoy. You can also go to the homepage and press Surprise me.
